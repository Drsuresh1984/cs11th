About the Author

Dr. Suresh Kumar Sharma BCA,B.Ed,MCA,PhD,NET,SLET

Dr. Suresh Kumar Sharma is a Lecturer in Computer Science with more than 8 years of teaching experience. He is passionate about computer science education, emerging technologies, programming, artificial intelligence and innovative digital learning. He has academic qualifications in Computer Applications and Computer Science and is actively involved in teaching, research and educational technology. His approach focuses on making complex computing concepts simple, practical and student-friendly. He encourages learners to develop logical thinking, problem-solving skills and creativity. Through teaching, research and digital content development, he aims to contribute to quality computer science education.
